How it works

Powerful AI tools to find and manage your ideal donors

⚡

AI-Powered Matching

Our matching engine uses semantic similarity, cause alignment, and geographic overlap to find donors whose giving priorities align with your mission.

💕

Swipe Interface

Review AI-curated donor profiles one at a time. Swipe right on promising matches to add them to your pipeline, or left to pass. The system learns from your preferences.

📋

Pipeline Management

Track donors through a kanban-style board from discovery to outreach to funded. Add notes, set stages, and manage your entire fundraising pipeline.

🔍

Deep Donor Research

Get AI-enriched donor profiles with grant history, publications, focus areas, geographic reach, and key contacts. Powered by multi-source AI research.
